## Disposition (owner's composite ruling, 2026-10-31)

The AUD-BF judgment found R-3 not cleanly buildable as specified:
the hub suite drifted after the RTC 1 freeze (catalog pagination and
result-recovery dialects changed post-freeze with no CONTRACT §9
version bump or dated decision). The owner ruled the composite
(hub `docs/DECISIONS.md` PR #251; mirrored here by AUD-BH, #377):
the RTC 1 freeze holds — R-3 executes under option (b) against the
frozen pin suite (`e76b1f8`) with the full named subset wired — and
RTC 2 is adopted as RTC 1's §9 successor, with this server's RTC 2
adoption as a separate later card.

- CI job `conformance` running `scripts/rtc-conformance-ci.mjs`:
  the pin suite against the real door + real DB + `emulate` fake
