# Unleashed Brand Spec — v1.2 (2026-11-02)

*v1.2: owner correction, same day — the master-gradient mark is the product
line's own identity, never a product's mark; Vercel Unleashed takes the Sky
accent like every other product (§1, §3). v1.1: the Library claims registry
(D-005) adopted as the canonical record of accent claims; §3 and §8 updated
to name it. v1.0 was the original issue, the same day.*

**Scope:** every Robinson AI Systems product in the "Unleashed" line —
provider-connector MCP servers that out-power the provider's official offering
(GitHub Unleashed, Vercel Unleashed, Dokploy Unleashed, Neon Unleashed,
Workspace Unleashed, and future additions).

**Out of scope:** original products with no provider counterpart (e.g. PenPal)
keep their own branding and never use this mark.

---

## 1. The mark — "the Break-Out"

A thick ring, snapped open at the upper right, with a single angular shard
bursting out through the gap at 45°. It draws the product promise: the tool
breaks out of the provider's limits.

- The **ring** is the constant. It never changes shape, weight, gap position,
  or color treatment across the line. It is how a user knows a product is
  Unleashed at a glance.
- The **shard** is the variable. Its flat accent color identifies the product
  (see §3). Shape and exit angle never change.
- **The master-gradient mark is the line's own identity.** The version with
  the shard in the master gradient (emerald → teal, same as the ring) is the
  mark of the Unleashed product line itself — used for the line as a whole
  (e.g. the website's Unleashed section) and **never as any single product's
  mark**. Every product, the flagship included, carries its own flat accent.
- The mark contains **no letters and no part of any provider's logo** — ever
  (see §6).

read-back after it lands. First landed claim wins a collision.

## 4. Lockup and naming

- Product name, always: **"<Provider> Unleashed by Robinson AI Systems"**
  in full on first reference (store listings, docs headers, connect pages);
  "<Provider> Unleashed" is fine thereafter.
- Wordmark: Inter, semibold, sentence case for the provider name; the mark
  sits to the **left** of the name, vertically centered, gap between mark and
  text = the height of the shard tip.
- Never set the product name in the provider's typeface or styling.
- Code/package contexts use the same names (e.g. connector titles), matching
  the existing fleet branding rule.

## 5. Construction rules

- **Clear space:** on all sides, at least the width of the ring's stroke.
- **Minimum size:** 24 px tall. Below that, use the shard alone.
- **Don'ts:** don't rotate or mirror the mark; don't close the ring; don't
  move or re-angle the shard; don't add drop shadows, outlines, or extra
  shards; don't place the mark on a busy photo; don't recolor the ring
  outside its gradient; don't combine the mark with any provider logo in a
  single lockup (a provider logo may appear separately, unmodified, where
  the provider's own guidelines allow).
- **Backgrounds:** Charcoal first, Graphite second, Mist for light surfaces.
  No other background colors without updating this spec.

## 6. Trademark guardrails (the protector section)

These rules exist because the first Vercel Unleashed draft (2026-11-02) was a
recolored provider triangle — caught and replaced before it shipped anywhere.

1. **Never incorporate a provider's logo, mark, or distinctive shape** into
   the Unleashed mark, a product icon, or a lockup — not recolored, not
   cropped, not "inspired-by" close enough to confuse.
2. **The provider's name in our product name is descriptive** (it says what
   the tool connects to). Keep it that way: our name and mark must always be
   more prominent than any provider reference, and nothing may imply the
   provider sponsors or endorses the product.
3. **Disclaimer, required** on every connect page, store listing, and docs
   front page, in plain words:
   *"<Provider> Unleashed is a Robinson AI Systems product. It is not
   affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by <Provider/company>."*
4. If a provider publishes brand guidelines, the stricter rule wins; check
   them before any new branded surface ships.
5. When in doubt, make it *more* ours, not more theirs — escalate to Chris
   before shipping a questionable mark.

## 7. Assets and production

- Reference renders live beside this spec (`unleashed-master.png`,
  `unleashed-<product>.png`, WebP twins). They are the approved design
  reference.
- **Production masters are SVG**, drawn once from the approved master render
  so every product's ring geometry is pixel-identical; export PNG at 1024,
  512, 192, and 64 px from the SVG, plus a 180 px Apple-touch derivative
  where a surface needs one.
- File naming: `unleashed-<product>-<size>.png` / `.svg` for products;
  `unleashed-master-*` is the product line's own mark (gradient shard),
  never filed as a product's mark.
- A product's OAuth/app logo, favicon, connector icon, and social avatar all
  use its own accent file from this set — no per-surface improvisation.

## 8. Change control

This spec is the single source for Unleashed branding *rules*. The single
source for *claims* (which product holds which accent, slug, and name) is
the Library claims registry, `designs/unleashed-claims-registry.md` (D-005)
in Robinson-AI-Systems/library, and the claim procedure in §3 is binding on
every agent setting up a new Unleashed product. Changes to the rules are
Chris's call; when he rules a change, update this file and the accent table
in the same pass, note the version bump at the top, and file any new claim
in the registry in the same change.
